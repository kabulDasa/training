## Critical Rules for This Athlete

### Powerlifting
1. **Squat volume is new** — start at 10–12 sets/week (lower MEV end); watch lower back fatigue
2. **Sumo lockout is the primary technical fault** — paused sumo and deficit sumo are the first tools to reach for
3. **Attempt selection is a learned lesson** — opener must be 95–97% of best training single; never sandbag
4. **Face pulls are non-negotiable** — always include; shoulder health is priority number one
5. **Lower back fatigue rated 7+/10** → reduce load or volume that session; do not push through
6. **Meet simulation** — full gear, filmed, exactly 1 week out; use bar speed to finalize attempt selection

### Streetlifting
7. **Powerlifting base is an asset** — upper body pulling strength will develop quickly
8. **Competition standards are strict** — dead hang start, chin clearly over bar; shoulders below elbows on dips; no kipping
9. **Bodyweight competency first** — confirm pull-up and dip AMRAP before prescribing load
10. **Muscle-up is a skill** — treat it separately from strength training; false grip and transition work needed
