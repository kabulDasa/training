Running is a supplement to lifting, not the goal. Keep it easy: you can talk in full sentences
(zone 2, roughly RPE 3–4/10).

## Scheduling (limit the interference effect)

- **Warm-up:** 5–10 min easy elliptical or bike before any lifting session. This does not count as a run.
- **Runs:** 2×/week, after **push** or **pull** day, or on a rest day.
- **Never in the 24 h before leg day**, and never before a heavy top set.
- Running after lifting (same session) is fine; running before lifting is not.
- Deload week = runs stay, lifting drops. Peak week before a test/comp = runs drop to 1 easy session.

## Progression (run/walk → continuous)

Move up a stage after 2 sessions that felt easy. Repeat a stage if you were out of breath or anything aches
(shins, knees, Achilles) the next day.

| Stage | Session | Total |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 1 | 6× (1 min run / 1 min walk) | 12 min |
| 2 | 6× (2 min run / 1 min walk) | 18 min |
| 3 | 5× (3 min run / 1 min walk) | 20 min |
| 4 | 4× (5 min run / 1 min walk) | 24 min |
| 5 | 2× (10 min run / 1 min walk) | 21 min |
| 6 | 20 min continuous | 20 min |
| 7 | 25–30 min continuous | — |

**Current stage:** 1
**Target:** 20–25 min continuous easy run by **2027-01-15** (about 8–10 weeks).

## Drills (add once stage 3 feels easy)

- **Strides:** 4× 15–20 s quick, relaxed, walk back. After an easy run, once a week.
- **Cadence:** short quick steps (aim ~165–175 steps/min); land under the hips, not out in front.
- **Calf/Achilles:** 2×15 slow calf raises on push/pull day (shin and Achilles are the usual new-runner aches).
