Advanced Tuck: Shift your thighs forward until your knees are roughly aligned over your hips to lengthen the lever.
One-Leg Front Lever: Extend one leg straight while keeping the other tucked.
Straddle Front Lever: Spread your fully extended legs out in a "V" to reduce the weight of the lever.
Half-Lay Front Lever: Keep both legs straight but bend your knees at 90 degrees.
