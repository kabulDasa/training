Pull Day · Weighted Pull-Up — Main ISF lift▼
ISF Standard: Dead hang start — arms fully extended, no shrug. Chin clearly above bar. Return to full dead hang. Pronated (overhand) grip. No kipping or swinging.
Cues
  • Depress and retract scapula to initiate — "shoulder blades to back pockets"
  • Drive elbows down toward hips, not flared out
  • Chin clearly above bar — not grazing it
  • Lower to full dead hang every rep, no short ROM
Progression
  • +1.25 kg belt when all sets hit rep target with ≥ 1 RIR on last set
  • Hold weight if last set was a true 0 RIR grind
  • Technical failure (no dead hang, chin not over bar) = missed rep
Powerlifting-background faults: Shrug-starting with traps instead of lats · Not returning to a full dead hang (short ROM habit from barbell work) · Grip drifting supinated under fatigue
Pull Day · Muscle-Up — Aux (banded progression)▼
ISF Standard: Dead hang start. Pull explosively — transition over bar without kipping or twisting. Full lockout above bar. Return to dead hang.

Starting at ~50 kg effective BW (heavy band). Progress through band sizes over 12 weeks. Goal: unassisted BW muscle-up by Week 12.

  • Phase 1 (Wks 1–4) — heavy band ~50 kg eff: Consolidate transition pattern. Advance when 3×3 is consistently clean.
  • Phase 2 (Wks 5–8) — medium band ~60 kg eff: Next lighter band. Brief regression expected — normal. Advance when clean again.
  • Phase 3 (Wks 9–12) — light band → unassisted: Lightest band, then unassisted attempts. 1 clean rep beats 3 scrambled ones.

The turnover: At peak pull height — shoot elbows back and lean chest over bar simultaneously. This is the learned motor skill, not a strength issue. Your lat mass from the 210 kg deadlift is not the limiting factor here.

Pull Day · Tuck Front Lever — Acc▼

Isometric hold that builds posterior shoulder strength, scapular stability, and straight-arm pulling — all directly relevant to the weighted pull-up and muscle-up transition. A progression toward the full front lever.

Position
  • Hang from bar, arms straight (no bent elbows)
  • Knees pulled to chest (tuck)
  • Press bar down to bring body as close to horizontal as possible
  • Scapula depressed and retracted — same as pull-up initiation, but held statically
Progression
  • Start with 3 × 5 s holds in strict position
  • Add 1–2 seconds per session when the hold feels stable throughout
  • Target by Week 12: 3 × 10–12 s clean
  • Advance to advanced tuck (legs more extended) when 12 s is easy
Push Day · Weighted Dip — Main ISF lift▼
ISF Standard: Full lockout start, no shrug. Shoulders drop clearly below elbows at bottom. Press to full lockout. No swinging or bouncing.
Cues
  • Slight forward lean — not perfectly upright (shoulder health)
  • Scapula protracted and depressed throughout
  • Control the descent — no drop-and-bounce
  • Full lockout at top — partial reps are no-lifts in competition
Depth note
  • Shoulder clearly below elbow = competition standard
  • Going excessively deep stresses the AC joint — reach the standard, don't overshoot
  • Depth confirmed at competition standard — ready to load from day 1
Common faults: Excessive forward lean collapse · Incomplete lockout at top · Shoulders shrugging up at the bottom
Push Day · Overhead Press — Aux▼

Builds overhead pressing strength that complements the dip lockout and shoulder stability. Also develops the pressing pattern for dip-phase of the muscle-up.

Cues
  • Grip just outside shoulder width, wrists straight over elbows
  • Brace core — rib cage down before pressing
  • Bar path: straight up, slight backward arc to keep over mid-foot
  • Full lockout at top, lower under control
Loading
  • Start at a weight you can do 8 reps at RPE 7
  • Add 2.5 kg when you can hit 8 reps with ≥ 2 RIR consistently
  • Aux lift — keep 2–3 RIR; this supports the dip, it doesn't compete with it
Leg Day · Competition Squat — Main ISF lift▼
ISF Standard: Hip crease below the top of the knee. Same depth requirement as powerlifting. Command structure varies by federation — confirm with your target fed's rulebook.

Powerlifting technique carries directly. Week 1 at 80 kg is an intentional conservative re-entry after the recent deadlift-focused block. Progress: +2.5 kg (one 1.25 kg plate per side) when all 3×5 complete with ≥1 rep in reserve.

Lower back note: Squat is trained 1×/week (Leg Day only). If lower back fatigue ≥ 7/10 before this session, reduce to 2 sets or skip. Pull and Push sessions that week are unaffected.
Leg Day · Romanian Deadlift — Aux▼

Hamstring and glute builder with direct posterior chain carryover to the squat and to the hip hinge strength underlying your 210 kg deadlift. Lower back stress is moderate — monitor fatigue and do not push this one when already tired from squats.

Cues
  • Hip hinge — push hips back, bar stays close to legs
  • Soft knee bend throughout — not a stiff-leg deadlift
  • Lower until hamstring tension stops the descent (not floor)
  • Drive hips forward to return — same pattern as deadlift lockout
Loading
  • Start at 40 kg — RDL technique is different from conventional deadlift
  • 3 × 6 · add 5 kg when all sets complete with ≥ 1 RIR on last set
  • Keep 1–2 RIR — posterior chain is already taxed from squats
